Systems Opportunity Audit

A diagnostic engagement.

The free fit call determines whether a meaningful opportunity likely exists. If appropriate, we discuss the paid audit engagement and arrange payment. The audit produces your prioritized Systems Roadmap and determines implementation scope.

Useful when a process feels slow, manual, difficult to scale or hard to see clearly—and you want a grounded recommendation before committing to a build.

Audit questions

Why start with a fit call?

It is a short conversation to understand the business situation and decide whether there may be enough value to justify an audit.

What happens during the Systems Opportunity Audit?

We examine how the work happens, where it slows down, and which steps fit software, AI or human judgment.

What do I receive?

A Kylix Systems Roadmap with the workflow, bottlenecks, opportunity areas, recommended system, priorities and estimated scope.

Is my audit investment credited if I hire Kylix?

Your audit investment is credited in full toward implementation.

What if Kylix finds nothing worth implementing?

If we cannot identify a meaningful opportunity worth pursuing, the audit is fully refunded. If you choose not to move forward, the roadmap is still yours.

Who benefits from an audit?

Leaders who want to understand a workflow or operational constraint before choosing tools or committing to implementation.

Do I have to hire Kylix afterward?

No. You keep the roadmap whether or not you proceed.
